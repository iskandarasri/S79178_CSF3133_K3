About the Event

This event is held to promote innovation and collaboration among UMT students.

The Speaker

Dr. Iman & Dr. Ily is a renowned expert in the field of computer science and innovation.

Featured Activity: Coding & Development Session

The Coding & Development Session is the heart of the hackathon, where university teams turn their ideas into working prototypes. Held in BYOD 02, participants collaborate intensively under the guidance of mentors, applying classroom knowledge to real-world problems. Teams are encouraged to focus on clean code, scalable design, and a clear demo that showcases their solution to the judges.
