About UMT

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a public university located in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu, Malaysia. Originally known as Universiti Pertanian Malaysia's Centre for Fisheries and Marine Science, it has evolved into a prominent institution specializing in marine science, aquaculture, and maritime technology.

The university is committed to sustainable development and ocean wealth exploration. Through cutting-edge research, quality academic programmes, and community engagement, UMT continues to produce competent graduates who contribute significantly to the nation's bioeconomy and marine conservation efforts.
